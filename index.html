Case Study 1: Specification & Execution

Rupeedot Tech: Dev-Ready Specs and Founder Vision

Role: Associate Product Manager / Founding Product Intern Timeline: Jan 2025 - Apr 2025 Context: Production B2B FinTech Platform

Outcome

Delivered 6 comprehensive PRDs with end-to-end edge-case mapping. Reduced engineering clarification cycles by [CONFIRM: 35% cut baseline] across bi-weekly sprints, keeping cross-functional teams aligned with founder goals.

The Problem & Ambiguity

The founders had high-level functional concepts for enterprise reporting and workflow modules, but lacked written technical scope. Developers were making unilateral assumptions about data validation, error handling, and permission boundaries, leading to rework and delayed delivery.

My Role & Approach

I worked directly with the founders to break high-level business goals into functional requirements. I held structured scoping sessions with backend engineers to evaluate schema constraints before finalizing documentation.

How I Wrote the Spec

To eliminate developer guesswork, every PRD followed a rigid structure:

  • Context & Business Objective: Why we were building this and the primary success metric.
  • User Personas & Permissions: Explicit access rules for enterprise admin vs. viewer roles.
